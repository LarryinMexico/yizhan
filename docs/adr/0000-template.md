# ADR-NNN: Title as a decision ("Commit Kafka offsets after each batch")

- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNN
- Author: (the owner writes every ADR by hand, in their own words)

## Context

What problem or question forced a decision? What constraints apply (time, cost, scale, what must never happen)? Two to five sentences.

## Options considered

For each option: one sentence on what it is, then what it costs and what it buys. Include at least one option you rejected and a real reason. "Do nothing" or "the simplest thing" is often a valid option.

1. **Option A** —
2. **Option B** —

## Decision

The option chosen, in one sentence, and the deciding reason.

## Consequences

What becomes easier. What becomes harder. What new failure modes exist. What would make you revisit this decision (a number, an event, a new requirement).

## Evidence

Commands run, test names, experiment reports, or measurements that support the decision. If there is none yet, say so and say how it will be obtained.
